These are the animation frames I created for my scroll driven animation project.
Said project doesn't have a formal home yet, nor does it have an entry in the Projects 
section of this site, but I plan on rectifying that in a future update.
In the meantime, this little guy lives on the [About page](/about).

There's 1 neutral frame:

<img src='/images/physics_animation_frames/neutral.png' style={{ display: 'block', width: '100%', objectFit: 'contain' }} />

 (for when the object is at rest) and 10 frames for each of the following:
- Upward force
- Downward force
- Upward splat
- Downward splat
