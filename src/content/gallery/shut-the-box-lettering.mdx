When I was creating my [Shut the Box game](/projects/shut-the-box), I decided that I wanted a way of communicating
a brief message to the player that still fit with the simple design and layout of the game.

In the end, I decided to use the space where I was rendering the dice to display the messages.
But that still left me with the issue of making things look consistent.
To solve that, I made "dice lettering" to go in the space where the dice would normally go.

# Letter Sets

These are letters I tried to make without using any diagonal lines. For a few letters 
(D, V, & X), I had to cheat a little, but overall, I managed to stick to it pretty well.

# How These Were Made
I used Apple Keynote to create these letters, as it has good tooling for just creating 
simple geometric shapes and then spacing them evenly apart.

I started by creating the basic parts I'd be able to use to construct the lettering.

I then just used different combinations of those pieces to create all the letters.
