These are images that I drew to act as placeholders when a page I'd created boilerplate for wasn't finished quite yet. The `UnderConstruction` component loads one of these images at random each time it's rendered. I could've stopped at just one image, but I thought it was more fun to have a bunch, and I was having fun drawing them, so I ended up with 10 in total.

As the site nears completion, however, I won't have any more use for these images, and they won't show up anywhere on the site, so I wanted to stash them here for people to be able to see them.
